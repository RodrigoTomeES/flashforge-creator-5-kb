**Maintenance Purpose**  
To ensure all functional components operate properly and to prevent parts from rusting due to insufficient lubrication during long-term use.

**Tools Required**  
WD-40, lint-free cloth or lint-free wipes, and grease.

**Maintenance Interval**  
Once every 500 hours

**Maintenance Steps**

-   **Maintain the Extruder Mount Positioning Balls**

1、1.Remove the PEI plate from the print bed to prevent any residual WD-40 from dripping onto it, which may affect print adhesion；  
2、Spray a small amount of WD-40 onto a lint-free cloth. Do not apply too much to prevent dripping during wiping；

![ball1.png](https://wiki.flashforge.com/resource/pictures/%E4%B8%AD%E6%96%87/creator5/other/ball1.png)

3、Use the WD-40-sprayed lint-free cloth to wipe away any old grease and debris from the three positioning balls；

![ball2.png](https://wiki.flashforge.com/resource/pictures/%E4%B8%AD%E6%96%87/creator5/other/ball2.png)

4、Apply an appropriate amount of grease to a lint-free cloth；

![ball3.png](https://wiki.flashforge.com/resource/pictures/%E4%B8%AD%E6%96%87/creator5/other/ball3.png)

5、Use the lint-free cloth to apply a thin, even layer of grease over the entire surface of each positioning ball. Repeat for all three positioning balls. Only a thin layer of grease is required. Do not allow grease to accumulate or form clumps on the positioning balls；

![ball4.png](https://wiki.flashforge.com/resource/pictures/%E4%B8%AD%E6%96%87/creator5/other/ball4.png)

6、Use the WD-40-sprayed lint-free cloth to wipe away any excess grease that has adhered to the surfaces around the positioning balls。

![ball5.png](https://wiki.flashforge.com/resource/pictures/%E4%B8%AD%E6%96%87/creator5/other/ball5.png)

-   **Maintain the Extruder Positioning Plate Needle Rollers**

1、Spray a small amount of WD-40 onto a lint-free cloth. Do not apply too much to prevent dripping during wiping；

![ball6.png](https://wiki.flashforge.com/resource/pictures/%E4%B8%AD%E6%96%87/creator5/other/ball6.png)

2、As shown in the figure below, use the WD-40-sprayed lint-free cloth to wipe away any old grease and debris from the surfaces of the six needle rollers；

![ball7.png](https://wiki.flashforge.com/resource/pictures/%E4%B8%AD%E6%96%87/creator5/other/ball7.png)

3、Apply an appropriate amount of grease to a lint-free cloth；

![ball8.png](https://wiki.flashforge.com/resource/pictures/%E4%B8%AD%E6%96%87/creator5/other/ball8.png)

4、Apply a thin, even layer of grease to the area outlined in blue. Repeat for all six needle rollers；

![ball9.png](https://wiki.flashforge.com/resource/pictures/%E4%B8%AD%E6%96%87/creator5/other/ball9.png)

5、Apply a small amount of grease to the locations marked in blue below. Each grease drop should be approximately 2-3 mm；

![ball10.png](https://wiki.flashforge.com/resource/pictures/%E4%B8%AD%E6%96%87/creator5/other/ball10.png)

6、Use the WD-40-sprayed lint-free cloth to wipe away any excess grease that has adhered to the surfaces around the rollers;

![ball11.png](https://wiki.flashforge.com/resource/pictures/%E4%B8%AD%E6%96%87/creator5/other/ball11.png)

7、 Repeat the maintenance process for the remaining three extruders, then place the build plate back onto the bed.

![ball12.png](https://wiki.flashforge.com/resource/pictures/%E4%B8%AD%E6%96%87/creator5/other/ball12.png)
